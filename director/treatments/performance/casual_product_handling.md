# Casual register for handling small objects, as visible behaviour

Loose, low-key, unforced. Each object gets its own small look-and-pause rather than one generic
"natural" bundle. Laban qualities are scaled values that compile to what the camera sees.

## Wording
```prompt core
His manner is {register}: {weight_word} {time_word} movements, {space_word} in reaching, grip {flow_word}. A short look down at each thing as he handles it, a glance up to the lens as the case opens, one faint nod once the earbud is in. Face alive but not performed: a blink, a slight brow lift, uneven small expressions, no fixed smile.
```
```prompt core short
His manner is {register}; {weight_word}, {time_word} movements; a look down at each thing, a glance up to the lens as the case opens, a faint nod once the earbud is in; face alive, no fixed smile.
```

## Failure signs
- Actor hitting marks: even energy, held smile, locked stare at the lens.
- Frozen face between actions; perfectly symmetrical expression.
- The same aliveness bundle repeated on every beat.
