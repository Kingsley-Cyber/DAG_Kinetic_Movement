# Run 001 — bottle selfie

## Ask

> A woman films herself opening a water bottle and drinking, 8 seconds, phone selfie.

Target model: seedance (profile all-unknown; 1,000-character budget as a guess).

## Intent brief

Locked by the user: one woman; she is the one filming (phone selfie); the action is opening a water
bottle and drinking; 8 seconds. Open: who she is, where she is, the kind of bottle, how the phone
is held, the look. Implied: a screw-cap plastic bottle (default "water bottle"); vertical phone
framing; a casual, real-looking clip rather than an ad.

The hand ledger forces one decision: a screw cap needs two contacts (hand on body, fingers on
cap) while a selfie occupies one hand with the phone. Options: prop the phone (both hands free),
one-handed flip-cap flick (changes the object), brace the bottle against the body (fusion risk),
or match-cut across the opening (hides the action the user asked to see). Selected: prop the
phone against something on the table; camera static with a faint settle. See `decisions.jsonl`.
