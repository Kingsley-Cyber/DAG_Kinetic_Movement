# Compiled Prompts — Vertical UGC Product Reveal

## Veo 3.1

Vertical direct-to-camera UGC video in a natural home setting. The creator opens with immediate eye contact, gives one short problem beat, then snaps the product upward from below frame and turns the label square to camera. The reveal is quick but the product settles into a stable 0.8-second legibility hold. Authentic low-amplitude handheld camera, crisp commercial timing, natural hand grip, no label warping, no extra products, no invented on-screen claims. Native audio only with approved script.

## Kling VIDEO 3.0

Use custom multi-shot only if product identity can be locked across shots. Prefer a single 6-second generation or element reference for product and creator. Shot timing: 1.0 hook / 1.1 problem / 1.5 reveal / 1.4 proof / 1.0 CTA.

## Adobe Firefly Video

Use first-frame or composition reference for the vertical framing and product appearance. Do not combine incompatible style/camera/reference options. A camera-motion reference is unnecessary; restrained handheld can be prompt-only or added in edit.
