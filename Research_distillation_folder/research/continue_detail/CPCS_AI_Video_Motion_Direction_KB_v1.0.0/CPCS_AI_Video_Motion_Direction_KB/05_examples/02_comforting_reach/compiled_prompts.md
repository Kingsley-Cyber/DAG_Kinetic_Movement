# Compiled Prompts — Comforting Reach

## Runway Act-Two

Use a driving performance video because microtiming, consent pause, gaze, and gentle hand compliance matter more than text control. Character-image mode is preferred when body/hand gesture transfer is required.

## Luma Ray 3.2 Modify

Use the performance source. Set Motion high enough to preserve the reach and pause, Structure moderate/high, and Poses rather than sparse Blocking when shoulder contact must stay aligned. Keep Faces enabled where identity/expression preservation is needed.

## Veo 3.1

A quiet medium two-shot. The giver studies the receiver’s face, opens one hand, reaches slowly on an exhale, then pauses just before touching the receiver’s shoulder. Only after the receiver remains receptive does the palm make soft contact and yield to the shoulder. No gripping and no forced embrace. Small concerned brow movement softens into a restrained reassuring smile. Very slow push-in; natural body balance and realistic hand contact.
