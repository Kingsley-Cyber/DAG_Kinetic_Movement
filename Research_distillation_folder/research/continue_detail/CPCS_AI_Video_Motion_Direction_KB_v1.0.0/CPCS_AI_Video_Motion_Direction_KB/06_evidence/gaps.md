# Research and Engineering Gaps

1. A large, open, full-BESS dataset with multi-rater expert annotations, calibrated video/3D motion, contacts, and diverse domains/cultures.
2. Reliable visual inference of LMA Weight and Flow with uncertainty and target-response/contact evidence.
3. A validated cross-cultural intent-to-motion benchmark that separates production culture, audience culture, context, and relationship.
4. Standard continuous contact topology for multi-human and human-object video generation evaluation.
5. Open FACS-compatible expression timing data spanning nuanced, masked, asymmetric, and conversational behavior under clear rights.
6. Model-provider structured controls for phase timing, actor trajectories, contacts, and facial tracks.
7. Stable first-party API documentation/parity for some current video-model UI controls.
8. A causal evaluation metric for contact/reaction order that correlates with expert judgment.
9. Verification of the aliases `LabanWRML`, `ChoosenMove`, and the intended `CMD` dataset. These cannot be ruled out with 100% certainty; no reliable match was established.
10. Measured style-transfer benchmarks that score invariant action/contact preservation separately from stylistic similarity.
