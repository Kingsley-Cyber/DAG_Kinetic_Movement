# Contradictions and Non-Equivalences

## Basic Six vs connectivity patterns

The query’s six listed “fundamental patterns” align partly with Bartenieff exercises, while its connectivity list aligns with Hackney’s developmental patterns. CPCS stores two inventories and forbids automatic `SAME_AS` edges.

## FACS “44 AUs”

Public sources and implementations differ in whether they count facial AUs, action descriptors, head/eye positions, visibility, and gross behaviors. CPCS uses grouped source codes and dataset profiles.

## FACS intensity

FACS A–E is ordinal; OpenFace 0–5 is a detector output for a supported subset. CPCS’s unit mapping is convenient but not psychometric equivalence.

## Seven-phase grammar

Gesture, BML, animation, and biomechanics have related but nonidentical phase structures. The CPCS seven-phase sequence is a synthesis and permits skip/merge/interruption.

## LMA Weight vs physical force

They correlate perceptually in some contexts but are not the same construct. No direct conversion to newtons is allowed.

## Product UI vs API

A control shown in a product guide or web UI may be absent or differently constrained in a first-party API or partner wrapper. Adapters are surface specific.

## Sora status

Older comparisons may list Sora 2 as a current target. The official 2026 discontinuation notice makes it legacy/deprecating for this snapshot.
