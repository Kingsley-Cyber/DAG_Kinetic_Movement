# Source Quality Assessment

## Highest authority

Definitive manuals/specifications, official product/API documentation, foundational framework texts, and official dataset/software documentation. They control terminology and current capability claims.

## Strong empirical support

Peer-reviewed papers with accessible methods, data/protocol, and limitations. Their findings are scoped to population, capture, task, and evaluation.

## Emerging evidence

Recent preprints such as MagicFace and LaMoGen are included for state-of-the-art awareness but cannot carry the same weight as replicated peer-reviewed evidence.

## Practice sources

Film/animation books and professional conventions are valuable for production grammar but are not biomechanical or psychological laws.

## Quarantined

Unverified aliases, unsourced numeric tables, aggregator capability claims when official sources exist, and deterministic deception/emotion claims.
