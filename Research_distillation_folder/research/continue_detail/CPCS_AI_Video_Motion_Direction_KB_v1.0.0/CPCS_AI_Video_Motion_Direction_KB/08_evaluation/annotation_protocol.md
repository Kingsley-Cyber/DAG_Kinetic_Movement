# Human Annotation Protocol

## Preparation

Annotators receive the canonical intent and observable acceptance criteria but not model/provider/condition. They may replay, scrub, and view frame numbers. They do not infer hidden emotion or deception.

## Procedure

1. Mark scene duration and observable actors/objects.
2. Mark action/interaction phase landmarks and contacts.
3. Score action predicate and causal order.
4. Score identity, body/BESS read, timing, expression/gaze, camera, style, physics, and usefulness.
5. Add timestamped failure evidence.
6. Mark criteria not observable.
7. Record confidence.

## Adjudication

At least two annotators per item in benchmark development. Disagreements above a threshold receive adjudication, but original labels remain stored. Report both pre-adjudication reliability and final gold labels.

## Safety and privacy

Use licensed/consented reference performances. Do not use identity or facial analysis for employment, policing, medical diagnosis, or deception judgment. Separate fictional direction from real-person inference.
