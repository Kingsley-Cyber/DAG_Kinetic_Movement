# Research Mission

Build a versioned knowledge base for the **Canonical Prompt Control System (CPCS)**: a capability-aware intermediate representation that translates creative intent into machine-readable motion direction and compiles it to model-specific controls and prompts.

## Requested scope

The repository covers fourteen research topics: full Laban BESS; Bartenieff connectivity; motion phases; FACS; kinematics; abstract intent; interaction predicates; force dynamics; camera grammar; rhythm; VAD trajectories; motion style transfer; computational Laban; and current AI-video control surfaces.

The supplied query ended immediately after Topic 14's intent line. Topic 14 is therefore reconstructed around the stated objective: identify native controls, prompt-only controls, reference inputs, output constraints, deprecations, and adapter implications for the major video models relevant to CPCS.

## CPCS target

CPCS is an engineering control layer, not a newly declared scientific standard. It preserves established vocabularies, labels CPCS-specific normalizations, separates qualitative motion from measured physics, carries uncertainty/provenance, downcasts canonical controls to each model's actual surface, and learns model-specific weights from immutable experiments without mutating curated definitions.
