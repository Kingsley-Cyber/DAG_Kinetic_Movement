# Corrections, Taxonomy Conflicts, and Scope Gaps

## Seven-phase motion grammar

`Initiation → Preparation → Acceleration → Stroke → Overshoot → Recovery → Settle` is useful for CPCS production, but no source establishes that exact sequence as a universal canonical model. CPCS labels it `CPCS_CONVENTION` and maps it to gesture phases, BML sync points, animation anticipation/follow-through, and action-specific biomechanics. Phases may be omitted or merged.

## Bartenieff inventories

The **Basic Six exercises** and the **Six Patterns of Total Body Connectivity** are different inventories. The former are commonly Thigh Lift, Forward Pelvic Shift, Lateral Pelvic Shift, Body Half, Knee Drop, and Arm Circle. The latter are Breath, Core–Distal, Head–Tail, Upper–Lower, Body-Half, and Cross-Lateral. CPCS stores both separately. [S002, Appendix B; S003, pp.54–219]

## FACS cardinality

Claims about “all 44 AUs” depend on edition and whether facial AUs, action descriptors, head/eye codes, visibility codes, and gross behavior are counted together. CPCS preserves source identifiers and groups rather than forcing one flat count. FACS codes visible action, not emotion, truthfulness, or deception. [S017; S018]

## Kinematics and force

Video-derived pose can estimate position and, after filtering, derivatives. Force additionally requires mass/inertia, contacts, boundary conditions, and a dynamical model or sensors. Measured force, inverse-dynamics estimates, visual proxies, and creative prompt priors remain separate. [S024; S026; S027]

## Culture and context

Intent/emotion mappings are probabilistic. The same motion may express different goals across contexts, and the same goal can be realized by different motions. Culture, relationship, setting, actor baseline, and genre are conditioning variables. [S033]

## Unverified named systems

No reliable academic or official evidence was found under the exact names `LabanWRML` or `ChoosenMove`. `MoveScape` appears in unrelated or practice contexts rather than as a validated automatic LMA detector. These names remain `UNVERIFIED_ALIAS` and are excluded from curated system claims.
