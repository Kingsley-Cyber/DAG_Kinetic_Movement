# Source Notes — Topic 06: Intent Vocabulary

**Sources linked:** 8

## Reading order

### 1. [S013] Behavior Markup Language 1.0 Specification

- **Authors/year:** SAIBA community (2011)
- **Authority:** `technical_standard` / `technical_specification`
- **Locator:** Behavior phases, sync points, and synchronization
- **Use:** Defines realizable behavior and synchronization; high-level intent remains outside BML.
- **URL:** https://projects.cs.ru.is/projects/behavior-markup-language/wiki

### 2. [S015] Gesture: Visible Action as Utterance

- **Authors/year:** Adam Kendon (2004)
- **Authority:** `foundational_research_text` / `book`
- **Locator:** Chapters on gesture units, phrases, and movement phases
- **Use:** Foundational gesture-unit and phrase analysis.
- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511807572
- **DOI:** `10.1017/CBO9780511807572`

### 3. [S031] The EMOTE Model for Effort and Shape

- **Authority:** `peer_reviewed` / `peer_reviewed_conference`
- **Locator:** SIGGRAPH 2000, pp.173–182

- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.1145/344779.352172
- **DOI:** `10.1145/344779.352172`

### 4. [S033] Cross-Cultural Variation of Speech-Accompanying Gesture: A Review

- **Authors/year:** Sotaro Kita (2009)

- **DOI:** `10.1075/gest.9.2.03kit`

### 5. [S032] Emotion Analysis and Classification Using LMA Entities

- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.1111/cgf.12598
- **DOI:** `10.1111/cgf.12598`

### 6. [S030] BEAT: A Large-Scale Semantic and Emotional Multi-Modal Dataset for Conversational Gestures Synthesis

### 7. [S071] Bodily Expressed Emotion Understanding Through Integrating Laban Movement Analysis

- **DOI:** `10.1016/j.patter.2023.100816`
