# Usage and Rights Notice

This package is a research synthesis and engineering design, not a medical, psychological, legal, safety, or forensic instrument.

- Do not use FACS, micro-expressions, gaze, VAD, or movement qualities to claim that a real person is lying, dangerous, mentally ill, or experiencing a specific private emotion.
- Combat examples are staged choreography representations. Performer safety, qualified supervision, controlled contact, and production protocols take precedence over generated timing or force presets.
- Biomechanical ranges are illustrative and population/protocol dependent. They are not injury thresholds or training prescriptions.
- Vendor capability files are dated observations. Confirm the live first-party API/UI, model identifier, region, tier, limits, and incompatibilities before execution.
- Book, manual, paper, dataset, and software rights remain with their respective owners. The repository contains citations and original synthesis; it intentionally does not redistribute the proprietary FACS manual or the NRC VAD lexicon.
- Style labels are descriptive control profiles. Brand names, character identities, performer likenesses, voices, music, and reference assets require appropriate rights and consent.
